Frequently asked questions about Word & Character Counter

How are words counted?

Words are counted by splitting the text at whitespace, including spaces, tabs, and line breaks. Punctuation remains attached to its word.

What is the maximum text length?

The tool accepts up to 1 million characters. Very large texts may take longer to process depending on your device and browser.

How is reading time estimated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute. Non-empty text has a minimum displayed reading time of one minute.
